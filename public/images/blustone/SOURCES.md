# Blustone gameplay captures

Actual original-client captures from the local Blustone revival research build,
recorded on 2026-10-23. These illustrate selected reconstructed systems, not a
complete restoration or original production balance.

Only the game viewport is included in these PNGs. Emulator chrome, advertising,
and unused desktop areas were excluded; gameplay pixels were not retouched.
The preserved source captures remain in the Blustone-Server workspace under
`local/native-validation-20261023/`.
