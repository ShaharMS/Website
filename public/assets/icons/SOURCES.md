No image generator was used. The added artwork uses the existing monochrome palette.

- Monster claw: https://worldvectorlogo.com/logo/monster-energy-2
- Download: https://cdn.worldvectorlogo.com/logos/monster-energy-2.svg

Only the complete claw-mark subpath is rendered as an unfilled outline, in monochrome.
The normalized derivative is monster-claw.svg: the first continuous subpath of the
green source path, uniformly scaled to 17 units tall and centered in a 24-unit viewBox.
No clipping mask or lettering remains in the derivative; its padding allows wiggle.
The car retains the sourced Tabler body and wheels, with simple animated spokes and
passing road markings and road edges added for the requested driving animation.
Monster Energy's logo is a trademark of its respective owner.
The unmodified download is retained as monster-logo-alt.svg for source traceability.
